I participated in the **Real-World Reinforcement Learning Workshop** in Hannover, organized by [Marius Lindauer](https://www.ai.uni-hannover.de/en/lindauer) and the [Institute of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.ai.uni-hannover.de/en/) at Leibniz University Hannover. The four-day workshop brought together researchers from different areas of reinforcement learning and its applications to discuss the challenges of bringing RL into real-world decision-making problems.
