The preprint of our survey *[A Decade of Bayesian Optimization for Controller Tuning and Robot Learning: Tutorial, Review, and Future Prospects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09403)* is now available on arXiv. We review methodological developments and applications of Bayesian optimization in control and robotics over the past decade and discuss open challenges and promising directions for future research.
