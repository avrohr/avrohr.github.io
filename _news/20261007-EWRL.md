I attended **[EWRL 2026](https://ewrl-org.github.io/ewrl-2026/)** in Lille, France, and presented our work on *[Efficient Heteroscedastic Bayesian Optimization for Risk-Aware AutoRL](https://rlj.cs.umass.edu/2026/papers/Paper150.html)*, published at RLC 2026. Our approach uses adaptive replication in Bayesian optimization to find reinforcement-learning hyperparameters that balance high expected returns with low variability.
