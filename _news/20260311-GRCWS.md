I co-organized the **[Benchmarking Safety and Dependability for AI-Powered Robots](https://robotics-institute-germany.de/grc-workshops/)** workshop at the **[German Robotics Conference (GRC) 2026](https://robotics-institute-germany.de/event/grc/)** in Cologne. The workshop brought together researchers from academia and industry to discuss how to define, measure, and benchmark the safety and dependability of AI-powered robotic systems.
