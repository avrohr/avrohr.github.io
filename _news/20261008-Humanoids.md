Our paper on *[Synergistic Action and Style Priors for Learning Legged Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03246)* was accepted at **[Humanoids 2026](https://2026.ieee-humanoids.org/)**. We combine latent action priors learned from expert demonstrations with style rewards to improve learning efficiency and produce more natural legged locomotion. Videos and further details are available on the [project page](https://sites.google.com/view/latent-action-priors).
