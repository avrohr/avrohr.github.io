Our paper *[Efficient Heteroscedastic Bayesian Optimization for Risk-Aware AutoRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26680)* was accepted to **[RLC 2026](https://rl-conference.cc/)**. We introduce **ERAHBO**, a Bayesian optimization method that efficiently searches for reinforcement-learning hyperparameters with high average performance and low variability by modeling heteroscedastic outcomes and adaptively deciding when additional training runs are needed.
